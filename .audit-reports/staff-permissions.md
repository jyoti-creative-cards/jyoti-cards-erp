# Staff / Permissions / Auth / Recycle-Bin / Activity-Log Audit — JC ERP

Scope: `JC/backend/app/{deps.py, routers/*, services/permissions.py, services/cost_visibility.py, services/void_service.py, services/activity.py, services/history.py, services/ap_ledger.py, services/ledger.py}` and `JC/web/admin/{app.js, js/staff.js, js/debit-notes.js, js/*}`.

Tests referenced: `cd JC/backend && python3 -m pytest tests/ -q` (not re-run for this read-only audit; findings are from static code review).

## Technical Findings

### Critical

- **[critical]** `JC/backend/app/routers/debit_notes.py:112` — `POST /debit-notes/{note_id}/void` is gated by `require_permission("vendor_orders.write")`, not `require_admin`. Every other void endpoint in the system (`stock.py:903` receipt void, `customer_orders.py:875,996` bill/placement void, `customer_returns.py:107` return void) correctly requires `require_admin`. The "Accountant" role preset (`JC/web/admin/js/staff.js:143`) explicitly grants `vendor_orders.write`, so **an accountant-preset staff member (Nikhil/Fiza/Raj) can void debit notes** — directly violating "no delete/void/purge button" for this role. `app/services/void_service.py`'s own module docstring frames void/purge as an owner-controlled undo path, but this one entry point bypasses that.
- **[critical]** `JC/backend/app/services/ap_ledger.py:561` (`build_ap_ledger`, inside the `details.debit_note` block) — emits `"unit_price": format(dn.unit_price, "f")` **without** calling `hide_cost()`, unlike the equivalent field in `app/routers/debit_notes.py:52`. This function has no `auth` parameter at all, so it cannot redact. It's consumed by:
  - `GET /accounts-payable/vendor/{vendor_id}` (`accounts_payable.py:47`, gated only by `ap.read`) → surfaces via `ApVendorDetail.entries[].details` and, after `build_ap_statement`'s merge (`ap_ledger.py:795-818`), via `ApVendorDetail.bills[].debit_notes[].unit_price`.
  - Both `ApLedgerEntryOut.details: dict = {}` (`schemas/accounts_payable.py:31`) and `ApVendorDetail.bills: List[dict] = []` (`schemas/accounts_payable.py:62`) are untyped passthrough dicts, so Pydantic does not (and cannot) strip the field.
  - **This is the exact "Nikhil" scenario from the brief**: he was granted `ap.read`/`ap.write` for outstanding/bills/payments only, explicitly *not* buying-price visibility — but this endpoint leaks the debit note's `unit_price` (a buying-price cost hint, per the comment in `debit_notes.py:51`) to anyone holding `ap.read`, with no `costs.read` check.

### High

- **[high]** `JC/backend/app/routers/recycle_bin.py:222-451` — `restore`/purge (`DELETE`) for **route, city, customer, vendor, catalog-product, addon** are gated by `require_permission("recycle.write")`, a normal delegable staff permission — **not** `require_admin`. Contrast with the same file's `staff`, `receipts`, `debit-notes`, `customer-bills`, `customer-placements`, `customer-returns` restore/purge endpoints, which all correctly use `require_admin` (lines 458-573). Per the stated convention ("purge … must be require_admin-only regardless of staff permissions"), this is an inconsistent, unintentional-looking gap: a staff member holding only `recycle.write` can **permanently and irreversibly delete** customers, vendors, catalog products, addons, routes, and cities with no owner involvement.
- **[high]** `JC/backend/app/services/permissions.py:26` — the "Recycle Bin" permission group bundles `recycle.write` = "Restore / permanently delete" as a single checkbox. There is no way to grant "restore only" without also granting permanent purge power. Combined with the finding above, ticking one checkbox meant for undo-mistakes access silently grants irreversible delete rights over 6 entity types.
- **[high]** `JC/web/admin/js/staff.js:130-134` — the built-in **"Setup" role preset** grants `["setup.read", "setup.write", "recycle.read", "recycle.write"]`. A business owner clicking this preset for a routes/cities data-entry role unknowingly also grants that staff member permanent-purge rights over customers, vendors, catalog products and addons (see the two findings above) — entities completely unrelated to "Setup".

### Medium

- **[medium]** `JC/backend/app/services/permissions.py:17` — `stock.write` is labeled `"Receive stock & edit prices"` but the only endpoint actually gated by it is `stock.py:479` (`update_stock_threshold`, i.e. the low-stock alert threshold). It does **not** grant price-edit or stock-receiving rights (those are `require_admin` at `stock.py:410/442/508` and `vendor_orders.write` for receiving). The permission label overstates what it grants, which could mislead an owner auditing a staff member's access, and understates the same for anyone reading the checkbox literally.
- **[medium]** `JC/backend/app/routers/debit_notes.py:126` — `PATCH /debit-notes/{note_id}` (edit) is also gated by `vendor_orders.write` (consistent with create), which is fine on its own, but because void shares the same gate (see Critical #1) an accountant editing a debit note and an accountant voiding one are indistinguishable in the permission model even though void has materially higher blast radius (recycle-bin move + reversal of stock/AP effects).
- **[medium]** `JC/backend/app/routers/staff.py` — no endpoint exists to reset/change a staff member's password (`StaffUpdate` in `schemas/staff.py:26-29` has no password field, and there is no `/staff/{id}/reset-password` analogous to `customers.py:511`). If a staff member forgets their password, the only recovery path is deleting and recreating the account (losing the phone-number binding until purge, plus a new WhatsApp send).

### Low

- **[low]** `JC/backend/app/services/ledger.py:290,343` — `LedgerLineDetail.buying_price` is populated from `ln.unit_price` (the customer's **selling** price) in `build_customer_ledger`, reusing the same field name that means "our cost" everywhere else in the schema (`build_vendor_ledger` at lines 67/144 correctly puts the real buying price there, redacted via `hide_cost`). Not an actual leak today (selling price is not secret), but the field-name overload is a latent trap: a future refactor that generically redacts "buying_price" fields could either wrongly hide the customer's own price or, worse, someone could copy this pattern into a vendor-side path and skip `hide_cost` by analogy.
- **[low]** `JC/backend/app/routers/recycle_bin.py:289-303` — `GET /recycle-bin/catalog-products/{id}` and `GET /recycle-bin/addons/{id}` correctly redact `buying_price` via `catalog_public(...)`/`addon_public(...)`, but the frontend (`app.js:2128,2134`) renders it as `"₹" + p.buying_price`, which becomes the literal string `"₹—"` for redacted values instead of a clean `"—"`. Cosmetic only, but visibly different from how `hide_cost` renders elsewhere.

### Confirmed correctly gated (no finding, listed for completeness of the audit)

- `require_admin` correctly used for: staff CRUD (`staff.py`), staff restore/purge (`recycle_bin.py:458-480`), receipt/debit-note/customer-bill/placement/return restore & purge (`recycle_bin.py:483-577`), selling-price edit + bulk (`stock.py:410,442`), stock adjust (`stock.py:508`), receipt void (`stock.py:903`), customer bill/placement void (`customer_orders.py:875,996`), customer return void (`customer_returns.py:107`), AP/AR opening balance, payment reverse/void (`accounts_payable.py:78,233,264`, `accounts_receivable.py:79,252,284`), activity log (`activity.py:17`), reports/ledgers (`reports.py`, all endpoints), finance overview (`finance.py`, all endpoints), freight agent CRUD, document management (`documents.py`), export (`export.py`), bill-series create/detail/delete (`bill_series.py`), payment-mode CRUD (`payment_modes.py`).
- `cost_visibility.hide_cost()` correctly applied in: `catalog.py`, `stock.py` (product listings, product detail, price/entity history), `addons.py` (list/detail, price/entity history), `debit_notes.py` (`_debit_note_out`), `services/ledger.py` (`build_vendor_ledger`).
- `finance.write` entry-only payment endpoints (`accounts_payable.py:165-217`, `accounts_receivable.py:174-224`) deliberately never return outstanding/totals — good design for the "entry-only, no totals" accountant requirement.
- Staff auth: bcrypt password hashing (`services/passwords.py`), in-memory lockout after 8 failed attempts / 15 min window (`services/rate_limit.py`), constant-time admin-key compare via `hmac.compare_digest` (`deps.py:44`).

## Functional/Wiring Findings

- **[critical]** `JC/web/admin/js/debit-notes.js:365-366` — frontend comment states outright: *"Backend requires vendor_orders.write for both edit and void — gate both the same way."* The Void button (`js/debit-notes.js:394`) is shown to anyone with `ctx.canWrite('vendor_orders')`. This is cosmetically "consistent" with the backend bug above, but it means the frontend faithfully renders a Void button for accountant-preset staff — there is no additional UI-side safety net. This is the direct user-facing manifestation of the Critical backend finding.
- **[verified good]** `JC/web/admin/app.js:2061-2063,2190-2191` (`RECYCLE_COLS`/`canAct`/`canActOnThis`) — the frontend's recycle-bin "Delete Forever" button correctly mirrors the backend split: `ADMIN_ONLY_TYPES = ["receipt","debit_note","customer_bill","customer_placement","customer_return"]` require `isAdmin()`, while route/city/customer/vendor/catalog_product/addon only require `canWrite("recycle")`. This confirms the High recycle.write finding is a genuine, intentional-looking design decision (not just a backend oversight) — the frontend was built assuming `recycle.write` staff should have purge rights over these six entity types.
- **[verified good]** Void buttons for receipts (`js/stock.js:1992`), customer bills/placements (`js/customer-orders.js:792,858,898`), and customer returns (`js/returns.js:161`) are all gated by `ctx.isAdmin?.()` in the frontend, matching their `require_admin`-gated backend endpoints. Debit notes (above) are the sole outlier in an otherwise consistent pattern.
- **[verified good]** Activity log tab (`app.js:77`, `setup-tile-activity` hidden unless `isAdmin()`) and Reports tile (`app.js:63`) are cosmetically hidden for staff and backed by `require_admin` on `GET /activity` and every `reports.py` endpoint — no gap between UI hiding and backend enforcement here.
- **[medium]** `JC/web/admin/js/staff.js:92-101` (`openDetail`) — the staff detail modal always calls `ctx.loadActivity?.({ tableId: "staff-activity-wrap", actorId: s.id, ... })`, which hits the admin-only `/activity` endpoint. This is fine functionally (the whole Staff page is admin-only, so the caller always has permission), but worth noting there is no separate lower-privilege "this staff member's own recent actions" view — activity is all-or-nothing per the `require_admin` gate on `activity.py`, consistent with the stated business rule.
- **[low]** `JC/web/admin/js/staff.js:225-227` — `openEdit` renders the Login ID (`phone`) as a disabled input with no visible way to change it and no corresponding backend support (`StaffUpdate` has no `phone` field) — if a staff member's number changes, the only path is delete + recreate, same as the password-reset gap noted above. Functionally consistent between FE/BE, just a UX dead end.

## PM/UX Suggestions

- **[critical]** Fix the debit-note void permission gate to `require_admin` (backend) and `ctx.isAdmin?.()` (frontend), matching every other void flow. *Rationale: this is the one place an accountant-preset staff member can currently perform a void action, directly contradicting the "no void button" design intent for that role — and it's an easy, surgical fix (one dependency + one button condition) with no functional downside, since accountants can still create/edit debit notes via `vendor_orders.write`.*
- **[critical]** Redact `unit_price`/`buying_price` inside `build_ap_ledger`'s `details` payloads (thread `auth` through and call `hide_cost`), and consider typing `ApLedgerEntryOut.details` / `ApVendorDetail.bills` as proper Pydantic models instead of raw `dict` so future fields can't silently bypass redaction the same way. *Rationale: `dict = {}` fields are a redaction blind spot by construction — anyone adding a new field to the ledger detail payload in the future has no schema-level reminder to redact it, and this one already leaks cost data to exactly the "extra AP/AR access, but not cost visibility" role the business explicitly wants to support (Nikhil).*
- **[high]** Split the "Recycle Bin" permission into two: `recycle.restore` (safe undo) and `recycle.purge` (irreversible, admin-only regardless of grant — enforce `require_admin` server-side too, don't just rely on the checkbox not being offered). *Rationale: a business owner ticking "let this person clean up the recycle bin" is very unlikely to intend "let this person permanently and unrecoverably delete customers/vendors/products." The current single checkbox conflates a low-risk action with a high-risk, irreversible one — this is exactly the kind of scope-creep the brief calls out for Nikhil-style incremental grants, except here it's baked into the permission model itself.*
- **[high]** Remove `recycle.write`/`recycle.read` from the built-in "Setup" quick-role preset (`js/staff.js`), or split "Setup" into "Setup (routes/cities)" without recycle bin access. *Rationale: one-click presets exist so a non-technical owner doesn't have to reason about individual checkboxes — but this preset currently smuggles in unrelated, high-risk purge rights under an innocuous "Setup" label. A preset should never grant more than its name implies.*
- **[medium]** Rename/reword the `stock.write` permission label away from "Receive stock & edit prices" to something like "Edit low-stock alert threshold" so it accurately reflects the single endpoint it gates, and consider whether stock receiving (currently under `vendor_orders.write`) should instead live under `stock.write` for clearer separation of "vendor order placement" vs "warehouse/stock operations" concerns. *Rationale: an owner reading permission labels to decide who gets what should be able to trust the label; today it overstates what's granted and could cause either over- or under-provisioning by a well-intentioned owner who reads it literally.*
- **[medium]** Add a self-service "reset staff password" action (admin-triggered, WhatsApp-delivered, same pattern as `customers.py`'s reset-password/resend-whatsapp) instead of requiring delete+recreate. *Rationale: staff turnover / forgotten passwords are routine; forcing a delete-and-recreate cycle is unnecessarily disruptive (new activity-log actor identity, re-sending permissions, losing the "edit" history trail tied to the old staff id) for what should be a two-click admin action.*
- **[medium]** Add a confirmation step that explicitly names the *consequence*, not just the action, for recycle-bin "Delete Forever" — e.g. "Permanently delete this vendor and all history? This cannot be undone" instead of the current generic `"Permanently delete? This cannot be undone."` (`app.js:2232`), and consider requiring the admin key / a re-auth step for purge specifically (distinct from the softer void/restore confirmations). *Rationale: purge is the single most destructive action in the whole app (no soft-delete safety net left after it), yet its confirmation dialog is the same generic `confirm()` used for lower-stakes actions elsewhere — it doesn't visually or procedurally stand out as "the point of no return."*
- **[low]** In the recycle bin, visually distinguish "restore-only, needs admin to purge" items (receipts, debit notes, customer bills/placements/returns) from "restore-or-purge with recycle.write" items (routes/cities/customers/vendors/catalog/addons) — today both groups render identically until you click in and see which action buttons appear. *Rationale: a staff member with `recycle.write` browsing the bin can't tell at a glance which items they're actually allowed to permanently delete versus just restore, leading to unnecessary click-throughs or confusion about their own access level.*
- **[low]** Fix the `"₹" + buying_price` string concatenation in the recycle-bin catalog/addon detail view so a redacted `"—"` doesn't render as `"₹—"`. *Rationale: small polish item, but an inconsistent redaction rendering can look like a bug report waiting to happen ("why does it show ₹— instead of just —") and erodes confidence that redaction is working correctly elsewhere.*
